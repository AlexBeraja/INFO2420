In the Beginning

Rent A Pet began with a family that loved dogs. George and Carol Smith owned two Irish Setters that became beloved family companions.

While walking his dogs in the park, George noticed how many people wanted the experience of owning a pet without the responsibility.

Advantages of the Business

  • Access to pets without ownership
  • No medical or upkeep costs
  • Companionship and exercise

Disadvantages of the Business

  • Pet housing and feeding
  • Medical responsibility
  • Liability insurance

Where We Are Today

Rent A Pet officially opened in the fall of 1980 and has grown steadily ever since.

Today we offer a wide variety of animals including dogs, cats, pigs, horses, and even snakes.

What Makes Us Unique

Rent A Pet operates multiple franchises across Utah and employs trained veterinarians and animal specialists.

We serve customers of all ages and backgrounds and have an outstanding safety record.
